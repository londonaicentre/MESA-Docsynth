INTERVENTIONAL RADIOLOGY PROCEDURE REPORT

Procedure: Right nephrostomy insertion
Date: 22/01/2025      Suite: IR 1
Operator: Dr F. Mensah, Consultant Interventional Radiologist
Assisted by: Dr T. Grey, ST4 Radiology
Referring team: Urology (Mr Singh)

Clinical details: Obstructed infected right kidney secondary to 9mm PUJ stone. Febrile, rising creatinine.

Consent: Written consent obtained. Risks discussed including bleeding, infection, failure, damage to adjacent organs.
Pre-procedure: INR 1.1, Plt 210. Gentamicin 160mg IV given on ward.
Sedation/Analgesia: Local anaesthetic (10ml 1% lidocaine). Fentanyl 50mcg by operator.

Technique:
Patient prone. US-guided puncture of a lower pole posterior calyx with a 21G Chiba needle. Turbid urine aspirated and sent for MC&S. Contrast confirmed position in a dilated collecting system. Neff set exchanged over wire; tract dilated and an 8Fr locking pigtail catheter placed in the renal pelvis. Position confirmed with contrast. Catheter secured with suture and dressing, attached to free drainage.

Findings: Moderate hydronephrosis. Filling defect at the PUJ consistent with the known calculus.

Screening time: 1 min 42 s    DAP: 3.1 Gy.cm2
Complications: None immediate.

Post-procedure: Bed rest 4 hours, observations as per protocol. Flush 10ml saline if not draining. Urology to plan definitive stone management.
