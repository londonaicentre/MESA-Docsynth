Indication: This 71-year-old lady has end-stage osteoarthritis of the right hip with night pain and a walking distance of 200 yards despite analgesia.

Procedure: She was placed in the left lateral position and secured with pelvic supports. After skin preparation and draping, a posterior approach was used. The definitive components were then inserted.
