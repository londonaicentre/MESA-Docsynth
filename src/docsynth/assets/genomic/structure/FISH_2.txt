On metaphase chromosomes, this \r\nprobe hybridized as expected to the proximal long arm of both homologues of chromosome 22, however \r\nthe hybridization signal appeared larger on one homologue in the majority of cells. No hybridization\r\nwas seen elsewhere in the genome.\r\n\r\nThese results indicate that this individual has trisomy for the TUPLE1 locus, which is likely to be\r\ndue to a duplication in the proximal region of one chromosome 22 long arm. The possibility of mosaicism \r\nfor the abnormality has not been excluded.
