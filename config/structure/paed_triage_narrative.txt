Asked to review this 2y 4/12 boy (13.2kg) who self-presented this evening with his parents, triaged ORANGE. There is a 2-day history of fever, peaking at 39.4 at home and partially responsive to alternating paracetamol and ibuprofen, on a background of reduced oral intake — he is taking only sips and has had fewer wet nappies than usual (two today against a normal of six or so), with a single vomit this morning. No rash, no diarrhoea, and the picture is broadly coryzal. He is otherwise previously well, immunisations up to date, no allergies.

On my assessment he is miserable but consolable, alert and well perfused with a capillary refill under two seconds. Observations show a temperature of 38.9, heart rate 158, respiratory rate 36 and saturations of 98% in air; capillary glucose 5.1. There is no rash and no obvious focus beyond mild coryza, though I have not examined the ENT in detail.

My impression is a febrile toddler with reduced intake and early borderline hydration, most likely viral, but warranting a period of observation and a fluid challenge given the reduced nappy output. Plan is antipyretics, encourage oral fluids and reassess intake, repeat observations in an hour, and review with a low threshold for bloods and a urine sample if he does not improve or the source remains unclear. Safety-netting discussed with parents.
